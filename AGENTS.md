- The long campaign report is pre-rendered into 1,500px WebP slices and served by its dedicated material route, avoiding PDF rendering on mobile.
- The public Story maker processes uploaded photos entirely in-browser and never sends them to storage or server functions, preserving voter privacy.
- The app's MCP server lives in src/lib/mcp (one defineTool per file, registered in index.ts); the /mcp route and its OAuth metadata route are generated by the Vite plugin and must never be hand-edited.
- The OAuth consent screen must stay at src/routes/[.]lovable.oauth.consent.tsx: the [.] escape is what makes the dotted URL segment generate, and the loader reads the authorization id from location.search because the loader context has no search property.
